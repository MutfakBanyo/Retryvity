Max Heavy Finder

Find the geometry objects responsible for the highest polygon load in your 3ds Max scene.

Max Heavy Finder scans your scene and ranks geometry by triangle count and total scene impact. Instanced geometry is grouped so you can immediately see both the weight of a single object and the combined impact of all its instances.

Features:
- Triangle count
- Scene percentage
- Instance detection
- Instance scene impact
- Top 10 / 25 / 50 filtering
- 100K / 500K / 1M thresholds
- Select and zoom directly to heavy objects
- Renderer independent
- Read-only - does not modify your scene

Installation:
Copy MaxHeavyFinder.mcr into your 3ds Max usermacros folder, restart 3ds Max, then assign it from Customize > Customize User Interface, category "Max Heavy Finder".
